Cutee Birthday Website — Final

Open index.html after extracting the whole folder.

Updates in this version:
- Background music uses the requested YouTube track (video ID Sv1U-KTpiUA).
- Music is intentionally kept low at 14% player volume.
- A floating mute/unmute music button is included.
- Browsers may block sound autoplay; the first normal tap/click starts the music automatically at low volume.
- Added an interactive balloon-popping game with funny cat faces and emoji bursts.
- Retains the phone-friendly layout, final letter popup, photos, and floating kawaii cats.

Because the music is embedded from YouTube rather than copied into the ZIP, the website needs an internet connection for the music and YouTube IFrame API.


BACKGROUND MUSIC
The website now includes the supplied background audio locally at audio/cutee-background.webm. Playback is intentionally set to 14% volume, with a mute/unmute button. Browsers may block audible autoplay until the visitor interacts with the page.
